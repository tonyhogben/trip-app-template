What is this?

Start here

A private app for our trip, built by all of us

It starts simple: the agenda and the crew. Then, during the trip, anyone can send a feature request. Each request goes straight to the AI agent, which builds it and ships it live, often within minutes. The app grows into whatever this group wants it to be.

  1. Check the agendaThe bar at the bottom always shows what is on now and what is up next.
  2. Tap "I'm here"Check in at a stop so everyone knows where the crew is.
  3. Ask for anythingType or record an idea in Requests. Watch it go from Open to Building to Done.
  4. Reload when askedWhen a new version ships you get a Reload prompt. That is your feature arriving.

Make it yours

This app is a template. Out of the box it is deliberately plain: black and white, placeholder plans, placeholder people. That is the point. The crew makes it theirs.

Add a themePhoto wallQuote wallMap of our stopsGamesLeaderboardAnything you can describe

Setting this up for a new trip? Follow the checklist below, or hand AGENTS.md to your AI agent.

  • Agenda, trip name and dates in src/config.js
  • Crew first names in src/config.js
  • Passcode hash and KV namespace in Cloudflare
  • Webhook to your AI agent (optional, but it is the magic)
  • Group chat link and a first theme

Add your own theme
  1. Tap an empty slot, or send a request like "Add a neon theme with pink headings".
  2. The AI agent adds a [data-theme="your-id"] block of colour variables to src/styles.css.
  3. It registers the theme in TRIP_CONFIG.themes and it appears here.
