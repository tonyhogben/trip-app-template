# Ideas: things crews have built

The original trip started as a plain itinerary. Over a few days the crew sent
dozens of feature requests and the agent built all of these. None of them ship in
the template (they were specific to that trip), but they show what is possible.
Copy any of them as a feature request.

## Trip tools
- Map of every stop with a photo "visual tour", a "Where am I?" button, and
  "Log a spot" for places off the itinerary
- Hide or show past agenda items
- Ticket viewer for a booked activity (keep real tickets out of public repos)
- A big send-off message on the last day
- Travel card: a personal "boarding pass" for the trip to save and share

## Photos and memories
- Trip roll: upload photos (compressed on the phone first) and see everyone's
- Polaroid camera with a printed-frame look
- Daily photo challenge where the crew votes for a winner
- The bot rates every photo with a score and a one-liner
- Quote wall for the best one-liners of the trip

## Crew fun
- Fact of the hour about the destination, the same for everyone, with history
- Watch list of film and TV picks with ratings
- Sightseeing bingo card
- Soundboard of strange local characters
- "Shout" button that yells a phrase in the local language
- Fake voice: play a message in robot, chipmunk or movie-trailer voices
- Theme tune toggle
- Prescription shake: shake the phone for a random crew mission
- Mini games (Snake, Invaders, Breakout) and a crew leaderboard
- DJ deck with beats, air horns and rewinds
- Trip mode: a loud psychedelic light and sound show (opt in)
- Hive Mind: every phone syncs into one light show using a server clock
- Pixel-art crew sprites that run along the page
- A "look inside the bot" page showing how the agent works
- Feature creature: suggests a random feature idea to request
- Live price checker for something the crew followed

## Safety and comms
- SOS screen with flashing HELP and a siren
- "SOS a mate": alert one person, even if their app is closed
- Group chat button and local reminders before each agenda item

## Look and feel
- Themes: dark mode, neon rave, comic pop-art, a city-colours theme, a cyberpunk
  theme, a cabaret theme with its own music
- Colour-blind safe palette toggle with icons on status badges
- Requests kept secret (titles scrambled) until they ship

## Lessons from that trip
- Every always-on feature that polls costs quota. Make live features fetch on open.
- Loud or flashing features must be opt in and stop when the tab is hidden.
- Keep a short note on every done request so people can find the new thing.
